## Turning Around My First-Year Math Course

### What did I do?
I came back from a 13% on my first midterm in a first-year university math course. After that midterm I started studying hard outside of class and put a lot of time into actually learning how to do the math, not just memorizing steps. I got 80% on the second midterm, 80% on the final, and finished the course with an 80%.

### When did I do it?
First semester of my first year of university, fall 2024.

### What was good about it?
It was really satisfying to see my hard work pay off. Going from 13% to 80% showed me that I can learn something I'm struggling with if I put in consistent effort.

### Favourite memory
Pulling an all-nighter with a friend the night before the exam. We studied until the sun came up, then grabbed coffee early in the morning before heading to the exam. The best part was that the very last concept I reviewed that night ended up being the biggest question on the exam, worth the most marks. Walking in and seeing it felt like the whole night had paid off.

### What I didn't enjoy
The pressure after that first midterm was rough. Getting a 13% in my first semester of university made me doubt whether I could handle the course at all, and that feeling stuck with me until the second midterm came back. Catching up also took up most of my free time, so I didn't have much of a social life that semester. Honestly, I'm not sure that was a bad thing, since it's what got me through the course, but it did make the semester feel long.

### Grade: A
It couldn't have gone much better. I turned a bad start into a strong finish.
