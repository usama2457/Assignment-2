## Getting Through a Breakup and Working on Myself

### What did I do?
I went through a breakup that hit me harder than I expected. For a while everything felt worse: my motivation dropped and it was hard to care about school or anything else. Instead of staying stuck, I decided to put that energy into myself. I focused on my classes, kept busy with things that mattered to me, and worked on becoming a better version of myself day by day. Over time things got better, and I ended up in a new relationship that is healthier and happier than the one before.

### When did I do it?
2022

### What was good about it?
It showed me that I can come out of a low point stronger. Working on myself when I didn't feel like it built discipline and confidence that carried over into school and everything else. I also learned a lot about what I want and what a good relationship actually looks like.

### Favourite memory
The moment I realized I was genuinely happy again. Looking back at how low I felt at the start, and seeing how far I had come, made all the work feel worth it.

### What I didn't enjoy
The beginning was really hard. Life felt like it sucked for a while, and there were days when it was difficult to get anything done. Pushing through that without knowing if things would get better was the worst part.

### Grade: B
For the most part a good experience in the end. The start was rough, but I'm in a much better place now because of it.
