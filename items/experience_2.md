## Winning a Hockey Tournament With My Friends

### What did I do?
My friends and I entered a hockey tournament together as a team. We had a rough start in the round robin and came very close to getting knocked out. Going into the last round robin games, we needed another team to lose by at least three goals just to make it to the playoffs. It actually happened, and we got through. Once we were in, we played like a different team and ended up winning the whole tournament.

### When did I do it?
About two years ago, in 2024.

### What was good about it?
The memories I made with my friends. Going through the stress of almost being eliminated and then winning everything together brought us closer. It's still something we talk about.

### Favourite memory
The winning moment. After almost being knocked out and having to rely on another game going our way, actually winning the tournament felt unreal. Everyone celebrating together at the end is something I won't forget.

### What I didn't enjoy
I didn't like that we had to come so close to losing before we found our real potential. Sitting there needing another team to lose by three goals was stressful, because our fate wasn't in our own hands anymore. It made me wish we had played that well from the start instead of waiting until our backs were against the wall.

### Grade: A
Couldn't be better. We went from almost going home early to winning the whole thing with my friends.
