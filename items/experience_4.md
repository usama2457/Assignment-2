## Volunteering at the University Library

### What did I do?
I volunteered at the university library. I helped keep the space organized, supported library staff with day-to-day tasks, and helped students who needed directions or help finding what they were looking for.

### When did I do it?
During my time at Laurier, alongside my classes.

### What was good about it?
It taught me time management. Fitting regular volunteer shifts around classes and assignments forced me to plan my week properly. I also liked being part of the campus community and being someone students could come to for help.

### Favourite memory
Helping a student who was stressed and couldn't find what they needed before a deadline. Being able to point them in the right direction, and seeing how relieved they were, made the shift feel worth it.

### What I didn't enjoy
Some of the tasks got repetitive, and during busy weeks it was hard to give up time I could have used for studying.

### Grade: B
For the most part enjoyable. It wasn't always exciting, but it was rewarding and helped me build habits I still use.
